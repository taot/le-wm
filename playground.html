What differs

25-step: starts at a real expert state; the goal is where that expert was 25 steps later. One model plan covers 25 steps, so one good plan can reach it. Expert replays the demo.

Full solve: random start; the goal is the block on the green T, usually 100+ steps away. The planner still only looks 25 steps ahead each time and picks whatever lowers the latent cost soonest.
